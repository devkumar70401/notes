# Resistors color coding

## What those 4 color rings mean?

- First band: Digit 1
- Second band: Digit 2
- Third band(n): Multiplier ($10^n$)
- Fourth band: Tolerance (Gold/Silver)

> [!Note]
> - Fourth band is spaced a little further apart from the other three.
> - Hold the resistor so the Gold or Silver ring is on the RIGHT.
> - Read the rings from LEFT to RIGHT.
